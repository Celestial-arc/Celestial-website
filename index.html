📖 How to Use Celestial AI

Start a Conversation

  1. Open the app and tap the glowing orb or the Chat icon.
  2. Type your message and press Enter, or tap the microphone and speak.
  3. Wait for the orb to pulse and show a thinking animation; the response appears shortly.
  4. Tap the 👍 or 👎 below the response to give quick feedback.
  5. Long‑press any message for options: Edit, Delete, Copy, Highlight.

Use Voice Commands

  1. Go to Settings → Voice → Wake Word and turn on “Hey Celestial”.
  2. From any screen, say “Hey Celestial” followed by a command, e.g., “What's the weather today?”
  3. The Voice Assistant screen opens and the orb starts listening. Speak clearly.
  4. To create a custom command, go to Commands → +. Enter a trigger phrase and the actions.

Use Offline Mode

  1. Go to Settings → Offline Knowledge.
  2. Tap the language you want and choose “Download” (text‑only or full).
  3. Once downloaded, toggle Force Offline in Settings → AI Preferences.
  4. Now you can chat, search Wikipedia, and use the first‑aid guide without internet.

Download a Map for Offline Navigation

  1. Go to Maps → Download Region.
  2. Pick a city or country and tap Download (Wi‑Fi recommended).
  3. Once downloaded, say “Navigate to [place]” or tap the map and enter a destination.

Create a Secure Backup

  1. Go to Settings → Cloud Backup.
  2. Toggle Auto Backup on. Choose frequency (daily recommended).
  3. Choose a strong backup password. Do not lose it – we can't recover it.
  4. Tap Back Up Now to create your first encrypted backup.

Set Up a Recurring Reminder

  1. Say “Remind me every Monday at 8 AM to check inventory”.
  2. The app creates the reminder. You can view and edit it in the Tasks screen.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Celestial AI really free?

Yes. Basic features are free forever with fair daily limits. You only pay for Pro or Business.

Does the app listen to me all the time?

No. The optional “Hey Celestial” wake‑word listens only for that phrase, on‑device.

Can it download music or movies?

No. It does not pirate media. It controls your existing apps and plays your own local files.

What languages are supported?

English, French, Spanish, Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, Swahili, Zulu, Amharic, Kinyarwanda, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, and more.

Is my data safe?

Conversations and health data are encrypted on‑device. We never sell your data.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel anytime from Google Play → Subscriptions. Pro access continues until the billing period ends.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes! Download offline packs (AI model, maps, Wikipedia) and use it completely offline.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete Account. Everything is removed within 30 days.

Can I use the app on multiple devices?

Yes. Log in on your phone, tablet, and watch. Cloud backup keeps everything in sync.
