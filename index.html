📖 How to Use Celestial AI

Start a Conversation

1. Open the app and tap the glowing orb or Chat icon.
2. Type your message or tap the microphone and speak.
3. Wait for the orb to pulse (thinking); a response appears shortly.
4. Tap 👍 or 👎 below the response to give feedback.
5. Long‑press any message to edit, delete, copy, or highlight.

Use Voice Commands

1. Go to Settings → Voice → Wake Word and enable “Hey Celestial”.
2. From any screen, say “Hey Celestial” followed by your command.
3. The Voice Assistant opens and the orb starts listening. Speak clearly.
4. Create custom commands in Commands → +.

Go Offline

1. Go to Settings → Offline Knowledge.
2. Download language packs, maps, and Wikipedia content.
3. Toggle Force Offline in AI Preferences.
4. Now you can chat, search, navigate, and get first‑aid guidance without internet.

Download Maps for Navigation

1. Go to Maps → Download Region.
2. Pick a city or country and tap Download (Wi‑Fi recommended).
3. Once downloaded, say “Navigate to [place]” or tap the map and enter a destination.

Create a Secure Backup

1. Go to Settings → Cloud Backup.
2. Toggle Auto Backup on. Choose frequency (daily recommended).
3. Choose a strong backup password – do not lose it; we can’t recover it.
4. Tap Back Up Now to create your first encrypted backup.

Set Up a Recurring Reminder

1. Say “Remind me every Monday at 8 AM to check inventory”.
2. The app creates the reminder. View and edit in the Tasks screen.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions – Massive Detail

Is Celestial AI really free? What are the limits?

Yes, the core app is free forever with fair daily usage limits. You can chat, use voice commands, go offline, track health, and store secrets in the Vault without paying anything. The free tier resets daily at midnight UTC. If you need unlimited usage, advanced AI models, premium automation, or team features, the Pro and Business plans are available.

Does the app constantly listen to me?

No. The optional “Hey Celestial” wake‑word feature listens only for that specific phrase, and all detection runs on‑device. No audio is streamed or stored until you actually speak a command. You can disable the wake word entirely in Settings.

Can it download music or movies for free?

No. Celestial AI does not facilitate piracy. It can control your existing streaming apps (Spotify, Netflix, etc.) and play your own local media files, but it does not download copyrighted content.

Which languages are supported? How good are the accents?

The app supports 20+ languages: English, French, Spanish, Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, Swahili, Zulu, Amharic, Twi, Kinyarwanda, Pidgin English, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, Mandarin Chinese, Hindi, Bengali, Russian, Italian, Turkish, Vietnamese, Thai, and more. Vernacular voice packs add local accents (e.g., Lagos Yoruba, Nairobi Swahili) so the AI sounds like a native speaker.

How is my data protected?

Conversations and health data are encrypted on‑device. Optional cloud backup encrypts everything with a password you choose – we never have the key. We do not sell your data, track you across apps, or use advertising cookies. You can delete your entire account and all data at any time via Settings → Privacy & Data.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel anytime via Google Play → Subscriptions → Celestial AI (or Apple Settings → Subscriptions). Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

Can I use the app without internet?

Yes. Download offline packs (AI model, languages, maps, Wikipedia) in Settings → Offline Knowledge. Then toggle Force Offline in AI Preferences. You can chat, search, navigate, and get first aid without any connection.

How do I permanently delete my account and data?

Go to Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete Account. Confirm with password or biometric. All personal data is removed from servers within 30 days. Cancel active subscriptions first.

Can I use the same account on multiple devices?

Yes. Log in on your phone, tablet, and Wear OS watch. Cloud Backup keeps conversations, commands, and settings synced across devices.
