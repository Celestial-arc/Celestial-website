❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Celestial AI really free?

Yes. The basic features are free forever with reasonable daily limits. You only pay if you want Pro perks.

Does the app listen to me all the time?

No. The optional “Hey Celestial” wake‑word feature listens only for that phrase, on‑device. Nothing is uploaded until you speak a command.

Can it download music or movies?

No. Celestial AI does not download or pirate media. It can control your existing apps (Spotify, Netflix) and play local files you own.

What languages are supported?

English, French, Spanish, Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, Swahili, Zulu, Amharic, Twi, Kinyarwanda, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, and more.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your conversations and health data are encrypted and stored on‑device. We never sell your data. Read our full Privacy Policy.
