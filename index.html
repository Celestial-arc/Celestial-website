Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked

Is Celestial AI really free? What are the limits?

Yes, the core app is free forever with fair daily usage limits. You can chat, use voice commands, go offline, track health, and store secrets in the Vault without paying anything. The free tier resets daily at midnight UTC. Pro and Business plans unlock unlimited usage and advanced features.

Does the app constantly listen to me?

No. The "Hey Celestial" wake‑word feature listens only for that specific phrase, and all detection runs on‑device. No audio is streamed or stored until you actually speak a command. You can disable the wake word entirely in Settings.

Which languages are supported?

20+ languages: English, French, Spanish, Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, Swahili, Zulu, Amharic, Twi, Kinyarwanda, Pidgin English, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, Mandarin Chinese, Hindi, Bengali, Russian, Italian, Turkish, Vietnamese, Thai, and more. Vernacular voice packs add local accents.

How is my data protected?

Conversations and health data are encrypted on‑device. Optional cloud backup encrypts everything with a password you choose — we never have the key. We do not sell your data, track you across apps, or use advertising cookies.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel anytime via Google Play → Subscriptions → Celestial AI. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

Can I use the app without internet?

Yes. Download offline packs (AI model, languages, maps, Wikipedia) in Settings → Offline Knowledge. Then toggle Force Offline. You can chat, search, navigate, and get first aid without any connection.

How do I permanently delete my account?

Go to Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete Account. Confirm with password or biometric. All personal data is removed within 30 days.

Can I use the same account on multiple devices?

Yes. Log in on your phone, tablet, and Wear OS watch. Cloud Backup keeps conversations, commands, and settings synced across devices.

Can I change how the AI sounds?

Yes! PersonaForge offers 16 ethically‑designed AI voices with different accents, pitches, and personalities. Preview, download, and blend two voices together. All voices are synthetic — no real person was cloned.

Can I use Celestial AI without downloading the app?

Yes. WhatsApp‑Bridge lets you chat with Celestial AI directly through WhatsApp. Send text or voice messages to our dedicated WhatsApp number. Also available on the web at this site.
